RESTAURANT INSPECTION REPORT – COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH DEPARTMENT
Facility Name: Moonlight Garden Restaurant & Banquet Hall
Address: 455 Blossom Terrace, Santa Clara, CA
Inspection Date: April 12, 2026
Inspection Type: Follow-Up Inspection (Post‑Violation Review)
Permit Number: MGR-552901

SECTION 1 — INITIAL CONDITIONS
The inspector arrived at approximately 9:10 AM. The restaurant was preparing for the lunch buffet service scheduled to begin at 11:00 AM. Staff were present in both the buffet line area and kitchen. Ambient temperature in the facility measured 71°F.

Since the previous inspection on March 27, Moonlight Garden was issued three corrective action notices:
1. Inconsistent dishwashing temperatures
2. Standing water in mop sink area
3. Improper thawing of raw chicken

SECTION 2 — BUFFET LINE REVIEW
The buffet warmers were operational but warming trays displayed uneven temperature distribution. The left-most warming unit had a measured temperature of 118°F, while the right-most read 132°F. Food safety regulations require hot-holding at or above 135°F.

The following items were placed in warmers prematurely:
- Paneer tikka (temperature: 112°F)
- Chana masala (temperature: 108°F)
- Vegetable biryani (temperature: 115°F)

A staff member explained that food was moved early "to get ahead of the rush." This practice increases bacterial growth risk and violates the county’s hot-holding rules.

SECTION 3 — FOOD PREPARATION AREA
The primary kitchen was significantly cleaner than previous visits, showing evidence of improved compliance. Observed improvements:
- Cutting boards were properly sanitized and stored vertically
- New utensil holders installed
- Mop sink area cleared and dried
- Dishwashing rinse temperatures consistently maintained at 182°F

Remaining concerns:
- A bowl of thawing chicken left at room temperature on a metal counter
- Raw chicken juice dripping near vegetable preparation station
- Employee cracked eggs and used the same unwashed hands to adjust the stove dial

SECTION 4 — HANDWASHING COMPLIANCE
Handwashing compliance had improved overall. Three staff members washed hands before switching tasks. However, one cook moved from handling poultry to chopping tomatoes without washing hands.

One staff member kept hand sanitizer next to the spice containers and used sanitizer instead of proper handwashing. Sanitizer alone is not sufficient; soap and water must be used for at least 20 seconds.

Handwashing station availability:
- Station #1 (north wall): Operational with soap and towels
- Station #2 (prep room): Operational, but towels were nearly depleted
- Station #3 (near dishwashing): Sink partially obstructed by stacked pans

SECTION 5 — SANITATION AND EQUIPMENT
The inspector noted several sanitation issues needing correction:
- Grease accumulation inside oven vents
- Excessive steaming condensation dripping from overhead exhaust line
- Several food containers unlabeled
- Rice cookers with hardened residue along inner surface

Dishwasher logs showed entries for calibration checks, but times were inconsistent. The inspector advised the manager to standardize logs.

SECTION 6 — PEST CONTROL AND WASTE MANAGEMENT
Waste bins outside the facility were found with partially open lids. Visible fruit flies hovered near one bin. Staff stated the bins were “newly replaced” but did not demonstrate awareness about sealing requirements.

Pest control contractor report (submitted by management):
- Last visit: April 2
- Findings: No rodent activity; moderate fruit fly presence
- Corrective actions: Clean drains weekly, seal bin lids completely

Internal waste area inspection revealed:
- Wiping cloths stored on a cart near trash bin
- Excess moisture on floor due to inconsistent mopping
- Discarded vegetable scraps on prep counter near slow-moving drain

SECTION 7 — EMPLOYEE BEHAVIOR AND POLICY COMPLIANCE
Employees maintained generally positive compliance with attire rules. Hair coverings were worn by 90% of staff. One employee lacked a hair restraint and was asked to apply one.

The inspector reviewed the food safety training sign-off sheet. Eight employees completed training on April 5. Two employees (night shift) had not yet attended mandatory hygiene review.

Four employees spoke briefly with inspector and shared concerns about:
- Busy hours preventing adherence to handwashing frequency
- Difficulty maintaining food at proper warmer temperature during buffet service
- Inadequate communication between morning and lunch shift

SECTION 8 — SUMMARY OF VIOLATIONS
Violations observed during this follow-up inspection:
1. Premature placement of food in hot-holding warmers
2. Thawing raw poultry at room temperature
3. Cross-contamination risk with vegetable prep station
4. Insufficient towel supply at prep handwashing station
5. Improper use of sanitizer instead of handwashing
6. Fruit fly activity near outdoor waste bins
7. Temperature irregularities in buffet warmers

Management was instructed to:
- Use probe thermometers consistently
- Assign staff member to monitor warmer temperatures every 30 minutes
- Reinforce handwashing during shift transitions
- Install signage near buffet line regarding safe food temperatures

Facility is scheduled for another follow-up on May 3, 2026.
