RESTAURANT INSPECTION REPORT – COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH DEPARTMENT
Facility Name: Rangoli Sweets and Snacks
Address: 130 El Camino Real, San Jose, CA
Inspection Date: March 4, 2026
Inspection Type: Routine Compliance Inspection
Permit Number: RSN-440182

SECTION 1 — GENERAL OBSERVATIONS
Upon arrival, the inspector noted that the restaurant was open for regular business hours, with customers entering and exiting. The dining area maintained acceptable cleanliness standards. Floors were wiped recently, and tables appeared clean. Several employees were present, including cooks, cashiers, and an individual responsible for maintaining the display cases of sweets.

SECTION 2 — FOOD DISPLAY CONDITIONS
A significant portion of the inspection focused on the sweet display station. The display case contained confectioneries including jalebi, kaju katli, barfi, and assorted laddus. Several items displayed signs of dryness and discoloration, suggesting prolonged exposure to air. The inspector noted cracking on the surface of certain pieces of barfi and slight hardening on laddus. These conditions indicate that the items may have been left unrefrigerated for more than recommended durations.

Additionally, the upper display shelf was not fully enclosed. Two trays of sweets on the top shelf were exposed to ambient air without protective covering. Condensation was visible inside the glass case near the right-hand corner—an indicator of temperature imbalance. The combination of uncovered trays and condensation suggests inconsistent handling and possible microbial growth risk.

SECTION 3 — FOOD STORAGE AND TEMPERATURE CONTROL
A temperature scan of the refrigerated unit revealed fluctuating values between 45°F and 52°F. The acceptable holding temperature for ready-to-eat sweets is at or below 41°F to limit bacterial growth. Staff explained that the cooling unit “takes time to stabilize after opening,” but inspectors concluded that the equipment failed to meet minimum regulatory standards.

Inspection revealed boxes of raw ingredients stored near cleaning supplies, posing cross-contamination risk. A bag of flour was placed adjacent to detergent bottles. Baking trays with residue were stacked without thorough washing.

SECTION 4 — KITCHEN SANITATION FINDINGS
The kitchen area showed mixed compliance. Positives included:
- Dedicated handwashing sink with soap and paper towels
- Food-grade cutting boards labeled for specific uses
- Staff wearing hair coverings

Concerns noted:
- Excess oil buildup on fryers
- Improper wiping cloth storage (cloths left damp on counters)
- Unlabeled storage containers
- Dishes found drying next to open trash bins

SECTION 5 — EMPLOYEE PRACTICES
Two employees were observed handling ready‑to‑eat sweets with bare hands. When interviewed, they stated that “customers prefer fresh food touched by hand.” This violates county hygiene requirements. Gloves or utensils must be used.

One employee was observed touching a refrigerator handle and immediately resuming food handling without handwashing. The facility must reinforce hygiene training and proper handwashing frequency.

SECTION 6 — WASTE DISPOSAL AND VERMINT CONTROL
The restaurant’s waste disposal practices require improvement. There were minor signs of pest activity near the rear exit:
- Small black specks consistent with rodent droppings
- Visible beetle near the waste bin area
- Waste bags not sealed completely
- Drainpipe residue suggesting irregular cleaning

The grease trap outside the facility had mild overflow residue. This can attract pests if not managed.

SECTION 7 — SUMMARY OF VIOLATIONS AND REQUIRED ACTIONS
1. Temperature violations in food display cooler
2. Improperly stored raw ingredients and cleaning chemicals
3. Use of bare hands for ready‑to‑eat sweets
4. Evidence of pest activity near waste disposal
5. Uncovered sweets in upper display case
6. Insufficient sanitation maintenance in kitchen equipment

Corrective actions required within 10 business days:
- Repair or replace cooling unit
- Implement covering protocols for display trays
- Provide hygiene retraining for all staff
- Deep clean waste disposal area
- Adjust kitchen cleaning schedule to include fryers and counters
- Verify pest control service within 2 weeks
