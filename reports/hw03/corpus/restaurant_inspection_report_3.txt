RESTAURANT INSPECTION REPORT – COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH DEPARTMENT
Facility Name: Fox Chicken Sandwich & Grill
Address: 999 Main Street, San Francisco, CA
Inspection Date: May 18, 2026
Inspection Type: Routine Annual Inspection
Permit Number: FCS-382190

SECTION 1 — FACILITY OVERVIEW
Fox Chicken Sandwich & Grill is a mid-size dine‑in and takeout establishment specializing in grilled chicken sandwiches, fries, and salads. The restaurant operates daily from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM. The inspection began at 2:37 PM during active lunchtime operations. Approximately 22 customers were present.

The dining area was generally tidy, with trash bins not overflowing. Tables were promptly wiped by staff after customer departure. Flooring near the drink dispenser contained minor sticky residue, likely from spilled soda.

SECTION 2 — FOOD HANDLING AND PREPARATION
The inspector observed food handling operations at the main sandwich station. Staff were preparing grilled chicken, slicing vegetables, and assembling sandwiches.

Key observations:
- Grill operator used separate utensils for raw and cooked chicken.
- Raw chicken stored at 36°F, which is compliant.
- Cooked chicken held at 140°F on the warming tray — compliant.
- However, tomatoes were stored in a metal container without ice, reading 52°F, exceeding the maximum allowable 41°F for cut vegetables.

A bag of mixed salad greens was opened but not sealed after partial use. Exposure to oxygen accelerates spoilage and microbial growth.

SECTION 3 — CROSS-CONTAMINATION RISKS
Cross-contamination risks were identified primarily near the assembly counter:
1. Cutting boards stacked while still wet, which can promote bacterial growth.
2. Employee placed raw chicken packaging on top of cooked chicken prep table.
3. Knife used for slicing onions was briefly placed into a container of cooked fries.
4. Storage shelf above the grill contained cleaning chemicals and spray bottles.

Employees must maintain separation of raw and ready‑to‑eat foods at all times. Chemical storage must be relocated.

SECTION 4 — EQUIPMENT AND SANITATION
The inspector evaluated equipment sanitation:
- Sandwich grill: moderate grease accumulation around edges
- Deep fryers: acceptable condition but oil appeared slightly cloudy
- Refrigeration unit: working but gasket had torn edges
- Floor drains: residue buildup noted, requiring deep cleaning
- Microwave interior: splatter residue on upper panel

The manager stated that the fry oil was scheduled for replacement “later this afternoon.” Oil clarity indicated it may have been overdue.

Dishwashing station temperatures were mixed:
- Wash: 148°F (acceptable)
- Rinse: 167°F (acceptable)
- Sanitizer: 146°F (higher than recommended; sanitizer should not exceed 120°F)

Temperature logs were missing entries for three days.

SECTION 5 — HANDWASHING AND EMPLOYEE PRACTICES
Handwashing compliance was mostly satisfactory but not consistent:
- One staff member washed hands before changing tasks.
- Another employee handled cash, then resumed sandwich assembly without washing hands.
- Gloves were used but not replaced frequently enough.

A staff member was observed wiping sweat from forehead with gloved hand, then continuing food prep. Gloves must be replaced after contamination.

Hair restraints were worn by all employees except one line cook who had long hair uncovered. Manager corrected the issue immediately.

SECTION 6 — PEST CONTROL FINDINGS
Signs of potential pest activity were observed in storage and waste areas:
- Flour sacks showed minor gnaw marks.
- Two live ants spotted near the back storage shelf.
- Cardboard packaging left directly on floor.
- Grease buildup near outdoor dumpster area could attract pests.

The facility’s pest control log indicated:
- Last treatment: May 8
- Notes: “Monitor for ants near storage shelves.”

However, employee reports stated occasional “tiny insects near soda machine.”

SECTION 7 — WASTE AND WATER MANAGEMENT
Waste bin lids inside the kitchen were partially open. Food scraps were found around the bin edges. A mop bucket contained visibly dirty water and was stored next to food crates.

The soda machine drain tray contained mildew-like residue. Water lines needed flushing. Ice machine inspection revealed minimal buildup but required sanitizing due to seasonal increase in use.

SECTION 8 — STORAGE AND ORGANIZATION
Storage room observations included:
- Unlabeled spice containers
- Open bag of fries stored next to cleaning chemical spray
- Excess condensation inside cooling unit
- Unorganized shelves with improperly stacked items

Proper labeling and separation of chemicals from food items was stressed during the inspection.

SECTION 9 — SUMMARY OF VIOLATIONS
Violations during this inspection include:
1. Improper cold-holding temperature for tomatoes
2. Unsealed bags of salad greens
3. Raw chicken packaging placed near cooked food
4. Chemicals stored above food prep areas
5. Grill grease accumulation
6. Missing temperature logs
7. Ant activity in storage area
8. Open waste bin lids and food debris
9. Improper glove use and inconsistent handwashing

Actions required within 7 business days:
- Repair or replace refrigerator gasket
- Relocate chemical storage
- Deep clean floor drains and grill area
- Flush soda machine drain line
- Seal salad greens and cold‑hold tomatoes properly
- Enforce glove replacement policy
- Clean grease near dumpster area
- Complete temperature logs daily
